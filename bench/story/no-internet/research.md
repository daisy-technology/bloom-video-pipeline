Gate: **passed**. The premise (a global, long Internet outage is physically possible, with real precedents and published estimates) is supported by a peer-reviewed paper (Abdu Jyothi, SIGCOMM 2021), a National Research Council report (2008), and real events (Tonga 2022, Red Sea 2024, Meta 2021, Visa 2018). All sources were fetched on 2026-11-10; quotes are verbatim.

## Not used

- Top10VPN cost of government shutdowns ($7.69 bn 2024, $19.7 bn 2025): about deliberate national shutdowns, not a global loss; would mislead next to the global scenario.
- "99% of the world's Internet traffic": wrong; it is international traffic.
- "Missed Earth by 9 days": no primary source; "about a week" is used.
- "Telegraph offices set on fire": NASA 2014 wording; the more cautious 2008 wording ("paper on fire") is used.
